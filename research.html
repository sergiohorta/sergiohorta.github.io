Research

Experimental and numerical mechanics of advanced materials

My research is developed within the COMES — Mecánica de los Medios Continuos, Ingeniería de Estructuras y de Materiales research group at the University of Castilla-La Mancha. The common thread is the mechanical response of materials and structures under complex loading, combining advanced experiments with analytical and numerical modelling.

COMES research group

Experimental mechanics

Multiaxial testing

Design and interpretation of biaxial and multiaxial tests, particularly with cruciform specimens. Current work includes tension–tension, tension–compression and compression-dominated load states, specimen stability and full-field strain measurement.

DIC · strain gauges · cruciform specimens · instability analysis
Composite mechanics

Non-linear response of CFRP laminates

Study of symmetric angle-ply laminates whose response is strongly governed by in-plane shear, including pseudo-ductility, fibre reorientation, damage accumulation and the transition between different response stages.

angle-ply CFRP · pseudo-ductility · non-linearity · failure
Computational mechanics

Damage and failure modelling

Finite-element simulation at different scales, from representative fibre–matrix models to laminate-level damage formulations. Abaqus is used to analyse initiation, stiffness degradation, crack propagation and failure under multiaxial loading.

Abaqus · FEM/XFEM · micro/mesoscale · progressive damage
Full-field experiments

Measurement & inverse interpretation

Integration of Digital Image Correlation with mechanical testing to obtain heterogeneous strain fields, identify local phenomena and support the validation or inverse identification of numerical models.

2D/3D DIC · full-field strain · model validation

Shear characterisation and standardisation

Highlighted methodology

Biaxial tension–compression test · UNE 0074:2023

A central line of work is the determination of in-plane shear properties by means of a biaxial tension–compression test with a cruciform specimen. The methodology developed within COMES contributed to UNE 0074:2023 and to the BISHEAR proof-of-concept project.

The test is analysed together with conventional shear methods in order to compare the stress/strain state, experimental accessibility and suitability for advanced composite characterisation.

Iosipescu · ASTM D5379/D5379M

V-notched beam method used to determine in-plane shear properties of composite materials.

V-notched rail shear · ASTM D7078/D7078M

Rail-shear method used as a reference for comparison of the non-linear shear response and strength.

±45° tension · ASTM D3518/D3518M

Tensile characterization of a ±45° laminate, widely used to obtain the in-plane shear stress–strain response.

Biaxial capability at INAIA

The Servosis four-actuator system extends the available biaxial loading space and full-field instrumentation capabilities.

Experimental and numerical capabilities

Experimental

Quasi-static mechanical testing, biaxial loading, DIC, extensometry, composite specimens and structural test design.

Numerical

Abaqus/FEA, non-linear mechanics, damage modelling, buckling and stability, micro/mesoscale simulations.

Materials

Carbon-fibre reinforced polymer laminates, polymer systems, nanocomposites, lightweight structures and selected additively manufactured materials.
