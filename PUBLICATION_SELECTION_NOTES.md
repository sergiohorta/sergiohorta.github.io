# Publication notes · v0.3

The website now uses two layers:

1. `publications.qmd` / `es/publications.qmd` manually highlight a short selection of high-impact or particularly representative journal papers, plus the 2026 ECCM22 microscale communication.
2. `references.bib` contains a broader research-publication list assembled from the two supplied BibTeX databases, the previous website bibliography and the most recent 2025–2026 records available in the CV material.

The highlighted set prioritises:

- biaxial tension–compression and compression–compression testing;
- shear characterisation;
- pseudo-ductility and non-linear CFRP response;
- high-impact journals such as *Composites Science and Technology*, *Composites Part B* and *Composite Structures*.

Before the public launch, it is still advisable to compare `references.bib` with an up-to-date Scopus/ORCID/WoS export so that the section labelled “Full publication list” is fully exhaustive.
